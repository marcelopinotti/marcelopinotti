## 🚀 What I've shipped

- 💳 **Card & Pix payments in production:** idempotent records in PostgreSQL and an encrypted offline queue in Kotlin. Sales keep going if the internet drops mid-payment, and no customer is charged twice.
- 💬 **WhatsApp inbox from 300 to ~50k conversations:** cursor pagination and on-demand media. It opens in 0.4 s and loads the next pages in 0.02 s.
- 🔒 **Security audit of a 504-route API:** fixed 10 OWASP Top 10 issues and closed data exposure for 110k+ customers.
- 🤖 **GenAI sales agent on WhatsApp:** answers 97.7% of customer confirmations, with all price rules kept in the backend to block prompt injection.
